Cutouts from my overkill Cricut / eufyMake E1 sticker printing workflow. I'm pleased that I'm able to print on small pieces of sticker instead of needing to use entire sheets the way Cricut's software assumes. To achieve this, I (+ Claude Fable 5 / GPT 5.6 Sol) reverse engineered the way Cricut places its sensor marks and built them into a GIMP plugin that allows me to place them around images before printing.

I learned from this sticker that I need to be careful with tolerances. The little frills on the edge of the butterfly's wings didn't cut out cleanly because they are too thin.
