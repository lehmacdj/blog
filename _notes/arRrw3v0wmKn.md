Right lord has two calls including a triplet of Dora, indicating a hand worth ~12000 points. By the half value rule we should have a hand worth at least 6000 points + be in tenpai to chase here. Because we're 1-shanten for a hand that could be worth only 3900 points if we get riichi nomi, we should fold here.
