micromindfulness: moment to moment concentration / awareness, what most people usually mean by mindfulness

macromindfulness: situational awareness, [starlight (from 3 kinds of focus)](/xSQogx2OaLIq/3-kinds-of-focus), (autistic / ADHD) fixations, my fixation on knowledge management systems throughout the majority of my life

My thesis is that macromindfulness can be thought of as similar to mindfulness, and involves similar processes. I don't think I really have the evidence to prove this, but it feels like a useful frame in the sense that both are a continuous coming back to a thing, just on different time scales.

Micromindfulness can create macromindfulness by creating space to observe what is actually most important to you. Macromindfulness towards meditation or other mindful practices can create/train micromindfulness.
