> Experience: Temp full body sunburn
>
> "flushing"
>
> Niacin is a B-vitamin. Sunburn effects lasts 30-60 mins.
>
> You will feel: warm, cozy, itchy.
>
> you're not having an allergic reaction
> it's supposed to be like that
>
> -Handwritten on 500mg Niacin bottle at Lighthaven

I recommend it.

It didn't feel like a sunburn to me. You know the feeling of a limb falling asleep? It was like that but only 20% as strong, and a full body experience.

Taking it was [unexpectedly mindful](/shWm4speAqvp/5-unexpectedly-mindful-activities). I was afraid it would be unpleasant enough to incapacitate me, but to the contrary, it connected me to my bodily sensations.

As I edit this post, I just took another.

One tip: no matter how itchy you get, resist itching.
