Dandelion poof candidate.

Requires:

- satin (does fabric matter? Can you use other fabrics?)

- double sided tape

- floral wire

Instructions:

1. Tape one side of satin ribbons, make a long row of bits of satin ribbon lined up next to one another

2. Cut thick side of ribbon off to make it easier to fray the fabric

3. Frag ribbon, removing all of the threads parallel to the tape

4. Remove other side of double sided tape, wrap around floral wire

Ideas for improvement:

- soak in thinned down Elmer’s glue, shape to a specific shape

- use “fabric starch”
