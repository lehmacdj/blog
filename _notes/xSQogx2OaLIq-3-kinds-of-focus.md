I think this idea originates from James Williams's Stand Out of Our Light: Freedom and Resistance in the Attention Economy.

## Spotlight
The ability to have a sharp focus on a specific thing. What most people think of as focus, the most stereotypical kind.

## Starlight
Long term (thus far away like the stars) kind of focus on distant goals or longer term projects

## Daylight
The ability to figure out what you want / what you want to focus your attention on (i.e. you need daylight to see). This mostly depends on a healthy relationship with mind wandering and good mental health.
