*Words for when today is ambiguous.*

Use **todaymorrow** times after midnight, when you want to talk about or make plans with someone for the same calendar day, but after you've slept and gotten back up.

Use **yestertoday** times after midnight where today is assumed to mean todaymorrow, but you want explicitly refer to the previous calendar day.
