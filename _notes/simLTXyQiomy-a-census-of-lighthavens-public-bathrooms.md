There are 13 public bathrooms on Lighthaven campus. 5 have showers, 3 have bidets, and only 9 of them are pictured on the map.
![Map of Lighthaven, with water droplets representing bathrooms](</images/simLTXyQiomy-Generated Image April 09, 2026 - 4_50PM.jpg>)

Let's start with the bathrooms in Darwin, where I'm staying. There are none. I think some of the rooms have en-suite bathrooms, but mine is not one of them.

The closest bathrooms are in Cantor, both inside Inkhaven's winner's lounge. You're only allowed into the winner's lounge after having posted. Conveniently these have 2 of the 5 showers. Don't tell Ben Pace that I regularly sneak in here before posting for my morning shower.
![shower in Cantor](</images/simLTXyQiomy-EC811913-483C-4F3C-B3FA-2DD148B60022_1_105_c.jpeg>)
The dial on the faucet is reversed, first hot water, then cold. I assumed I'd have to take a cold shower the first time I showered here.
![shower water dial](</images/simLTXyQiomy-32A5D5D4-3D75-45A8-BB8B-BD277C4DDF73_1_105_c.jpeg>)
This bathroom has a very spacious floor plan.
![spacious floor plan](</images/simLTXyQiomy-BCE20490-7DDF-44DA-A19C-A9C0210338B4_1_105_c.jpeg>)
What is that little gray square in the corner?
![detail of little gray corner](</images/simLTXyQiomy-195937DE-8420-4180-AD42-C51A22A30EC8_1_105_c.jpeg>)
Toilet paper holders are surprisingly rare throughout Lighthaven, but at least there's always a large stack of toilet paper rolls nearby. Very efficient I suppose.
![No toilet paper holder to be seen](</images/simLTXyQiomy-DE971567-2A0D-4D14-B9B7-856F16AAE363_1_105_c.jpeg>)
